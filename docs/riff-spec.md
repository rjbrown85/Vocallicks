# Riff spec

Source video: "Master 5 R&B Riffs Every Pro Singer Uses | Building Block Method," Indie Artist School (Crystal Cherelle). https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=saWqsqZUy5M

Note data comes from her companion tool, Riff Builder (https://riffbuilder.indieartistschool.com), file `/building_blocks.mid`, 100 bpm, 4/4. It was spot-checked against the Quick Dip sheet music in the video at 1:20.

## The five blocks

Default set is E minor pentatonic: E = 1, G = b3, A = 4, B = 5, D = b7. Rhythm is in beats (quarter note = 1).

| # | Block | Notes (beats) | Scale steps |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Quick Dip | E4 (0.5) D4 (0.25) B3 (0.25) | 1 b7 5, going down |
| 2 | Swift Lift | B3 (0.5) D4 (0.25) E4 (0.25) | 5 b7 1, going up |
| 3 | Swoopy Swift Lift | B3 (0.5) D4 (0.25) E4 (0.25) D4 (0.125) E4 (0.625) | Swift Lift plus a quick turn that re-hits the top note |
| 4 | Quick Dip Trip | E4 (1) D4 (0.25) B3 (0.25) A3 (1) | 1 b7 5 4; the landing note can be short or long |
| 5 | Skip Quick Dip Trip | G4 (0.5, accented) E4 (1) D4 (0.25) B3 (0.25) A3 (1) | b3 skips down to 1, then the Quick Dip Trip |

Other Riff Builder sets: major (C4 in place of B3, B3 in place of A3), major pentatonic (uses C# and F#), and blues (adds A#3, the b5, as a passing note in blocks 4 and 5).

## Combos from the video

Riff Builder chains blocks at fixed pitch. In the video she sometimes starts the second block on a different note, so these are the Riff Builder versions.

1. Quick Dip + Swift Lift
2. Quick Dip Trip + Swift Lift
3. Swift Lift + Quick Dip Trip
4. Swoopy Swift Lift + Quick Dip Trip
5. Skip Quick Dip Trip + Swift Lift
6. Skip Quick Dip Trip + Swoopy Swift Lift (her favorite)
7. Swift Lift + Skip Quick Dip Trip
8. Swift Lift + Skip Quick Dip Trip + Swoopy Swift Lift

Honorable mention, which she calls clunky: Swoopy Swift Lift + Skip Quick Dip Trip.

## Key walk chords

Each key in a walk opens with the home chord of the scale: minor 11 for minor pentatonic and blues, major 9 for major, and 6/9 for major pentatonic. Every riff note belongs to that chord's scale.
