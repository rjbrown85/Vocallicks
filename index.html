01Blocks

Crystal Cherelle's five riff building blocks with their practice ladders, the key walk, moving the shapes, and her combinations. This is the original Riff Blocks app, unchanged.

The five riffs

Each riff has a five-step ladder. Tap a step to run it. Her tempo is 100 bpm, so the speed-up step climbs from your tempo toward that.

Key walk

Pick a riff and the range you want it to cover. Each key opens with one bar of the chord that fits the riff, then the riff plays four times. The walk moves up a half step at a time, and after the top key it comes back down to where it started. It uses the scale and tempo from Setup.

Move the shape

All five riffs are anchored to the same home note. To use them while improvising, you need to start a shape anywhere in the scale. Pick a shape and the piano runs it from each scale step, lowest start first.

Combos

Her combinations chain blocks at the pitches you've been practicing, so the join between two blocks often repeats a note or skips one. Get each join clean at slow speed before you echo it at your tempo.

Build your own

Chain up to six blocks

Tap blocks to add them in order. She tried a few orders that sounded disconnected, so listen for joins that flow.

    How the scale choice works

    Every chord implies a mode. A D7 in the key of G is D Mixolydian, which has the same notes as E Aeolian. Riffs use five-note pentatonic slices of that mode, and the slice matters. E minor pentatonic over D7 skips the chord's 3rd (F♯) and ♭7 (C) and includes G, which rubs against F♯. A minor pentatonic, a 5th above the root, sounds bluesy. B minor pentatonic, a 6th above, sounds sweet because it holds the 3rd.

    Over minor 7 chords the whole-step-up idea works well: E minor pentatonic over Dm7 gives the Dorian color. Over a major V chord in a minor key, like E7 going to Am, the key's pentatonic has G where the chord has G♯. That card gets flagged so you land on the chord's 3rd.

    Styles change the chords themselves. Blues turns every major chord into a dominant 7, and R&B stacks 9ths, so the cards update when you switch styles.
