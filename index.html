RiffBlocks

The five R&B riff shapes from Crystal Cherelle's building-block lesson, set up as drills you sing back to the piano. Take one riff through its five steps, then move the shapes around the scale, then chain them into her combinations.

Setup

Loading the grand piano…

Each run opens with four clicks. In echo rounds the piano plays the riff, then leaves you the same stretch of time to sing it back over a soft drone. The page can't hear you, so record a few rounds on your phone and listen back for the quick notes.

Keep riffs light while they're new and add Curbing-level volume only once they're clean. Stop if anything feels scratchy or tight.

The five riffs

Each riff has a five-step ladder. Tap a step to run it. Her tempo is 100 bpm, so the speed-up step climbs from your tempo toward that.

Key walk

Pick a riff and the range you want it to cover. Each key opens with one bar of the chord that fits the riff, then the riff plays four times. The walk moves up a half step at a time, and after the top key it comes back down to where it started. It uses the scale and tempo from Setup.

Move the shape

All five riffs are anchored to the same home note. To use them while improvising, you need to start a shape anywhere in the scale. Pick a shape and the piano runs it from each scale step, lowest start first.

Combos

Her combinations chain blocks at the pitches you've been practicing, so the join between two blocks often repeats a note or skips one. Get each join clean at slow speed before you echo it at your tempo.

Tap blocks to add them in order. She tried a few orders that sounded disconnected, so listen for joins that flow.
